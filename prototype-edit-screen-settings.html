Fixed by earlier decisions

  • Target period mode: weekly/monthly measurable habits only; calendar (0) by default, rolling (1) offered as "per 7 days / per 30 days" (#31 Q1).
  • "Assume none": "at most" habits only; on for existing and new habits (#4, #46). Switching to "at least" keeps the stored value (#46).
  • "Skip if I've logged anything today": measurable habits only, off by default, resets daily (#31 Q5).
  • All three are stored on every habit and ignored where they don't apply (#42 Q6).
